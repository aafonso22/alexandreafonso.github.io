#### Overview

This dataset provides a quantitative measure of social dialogue in 25 European countries from 1980 to 2018, with country-years as the unit of analysis. Its main variable is a four-point social dialogue scale, from no involvement of trade unions and employers to corporatist compromise, based on an original survey of 109 national experts.

It accompanies the article “[Determinants of Social Dialogue in European Countries (1980–2018)]({{< ref "/papers/p32" >}})”, *British Journal of Industrial Relations* 63(2): 341–353 (2025).
