#### Overview

This dataset measures social dialogue in European countries between 1980 and 2018. It accompanies the article “[Determinants of Social Dialogue in European Countries (1980–2018)]({{< ref "/papers/p32" >}})”, *British Journal of Industrial Relations* 63(2): 341–353 (2025).
