#### Description

This book, under contract with Oxford University Press, examines how the development of social protection shaped the birth of immigration control in Western Europe. It grows out of the NWO Vidi project *The Borders of Equality: Welfare States and Immigration Policies in Comparative and Historical Perspective*.
